# Alarm reliability on the Camon 40

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 04
Map: [Remind Me v1](../map.md)

## Question

Does a scheduled notification and a full-screen alarm really fire on the owner's Camon 40 under every condition the hard requirement names, and which one-time settings does that take?

A throwaway app with no model in it: schedule a notification and an alarm, then test each with the app in the foreground, swiped away from recents, after a reboot, after the phone sat asleep overnight, and in battery-saver mode. Record what fired, how late, and which HiOS settings had to be changed before it did.

The owner has to do the waiting and the rebooting. The answer is the table of results and the final settings checklist, or the finding that some condition cannot be met.
