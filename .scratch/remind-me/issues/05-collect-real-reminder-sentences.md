# Collect real reminder sentences

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: none
Map: [Remind Me v1](../map.md)

## Question

What does the owner actually type when they want a reminder?

This is manual work for the owner (HITL): write 15 to 20 messages exactly as they would type them into the app, without tidying them up. Cover the spread: one-time and repeating, notification and alarm, with and without a stated lead time, with and without a time at all, several tasks in one message, and a few that change or cancel an existing task.

Resolved when the sentences are saved in this ticket's answer. They seed the agent test set.
