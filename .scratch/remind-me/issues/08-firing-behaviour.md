# Firing behaviour

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 03, 06
Map: [Remind Me v1](../map.md)

## Question

What happens when a task fires?

- What a notification shows and which buttons it carries (Done, Snooze, others), and how long a snooze is.
- How the notification at the lead time differs from the one at the due time.
- How an alarm behaves: what the full-screen view shows, how long it rings before giving up, what snooze does, which sound and vibration it uses.
- How each delivery style behaves when the phone is on silent or Do Not Disturb.
- What happens to a task whose time passed while the phone was off: fire late, mark missed, or both.
- How a repeating task moves to its next occurrence, including when an occurrence was missed or snoozed past the next one.
- What happens to pending notifications and alarms when a task is edited, undone or deleted.
