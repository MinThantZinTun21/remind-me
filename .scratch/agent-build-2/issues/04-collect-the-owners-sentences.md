## Question

What does the owner actually type, including the new kinds of message this build must handle?

This is manual work for the owner: write about 30 messages exactly as they would type them, without tidying them up. Cover:

- One-time and repeating tasks.
- Custom days, such as "sunday and monday", "every day except friday", "weekends" and "all days".
- With and without a time, and with a stated warning.
- Alarm and notification.
- Several tasks in one message.
- Follow-ups that only make sense after the message before, such as "change it to 7", "make that an alarm" and "no, thursday".

Resolved when the messages are saved in this ticket's answer. They seed the test set, and they also answer v1's ticket 05.
